## Overview

Forests provide many forms of value beyond timber production.

They support biodiversity, store carbon, regulate water, protect landscapes, and contribute to human well-being. Our laboratory evaluates these values from ecological, environmental, economic, and social perspectives.

## Research Questions

- How can the multiple values of forests be measured and visualized?
- How do forest conditions influence ecosystem services?
- How can forest value be reflected in management and policy decisions?
- How do people perceive and use forest?

## Research Topics

- Ecosystem services
- Biodiversity assessment
- Carbon sequestration
- Landscape evaluation
- Sustainable forest management
- Social and cultural value of forests

## Methods

We combine field surveys, spatial analysis, environmental assessment, and social surveys to evaluate forest value from multiple perspectives.
