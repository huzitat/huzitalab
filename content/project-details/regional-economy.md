## Overview

Forestry and wood utilization generate economic activity through forest management, harvesting, transportation, processing, construction, energy use, and related services.

The economic effects of these activities extend beyond the forestry sector itself.

This project evaluates how the production and use of local timber contribute to employment, income, industrial activity, and regional economic circulation.

## Objectives

- Quantify the economic effects of forestry and wood utilization
- Evaluate the regional circulation of income and expenditure
- Identify industries connected to forest-resource use
- Compare local and non-local timber utilization scenarios
- Support regional forest and industrial policies

## Research Activities

The project investigates the relationship between forest resources and regional economies.

Research activities include:

- Analysis of forestry and wood-industry production
- Estimation of direct and indirect economic effects
- Evaluation of employment and income generation
- Comparison of timber procurement scenarios
- Assessment of local supply chains
- Examination of links between environmental and economic outcomes

## Methods

The project uses methods such as:

- Input–output analysis
- Regional economic analysis
- Supply-chain analysis
- Scenario analysis
- Statistical data analysis
- Interviews and field surveys

## Expected Outcomes

This research aims to support:

- Regional forestry strategies
- Local timber procurement
- Development of forest-related industries
- Employment and income creation
- Sustainable regional resource use
- Coordination between environmental and economic policies
