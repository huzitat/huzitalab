## Overview

Forestry and wood utilization generate economic activity through harvesting, transportation, processing, construction, and related local industries.

Our laboratory studies how forest resources and wood-based industries contribute to regional employment, income, production, and economic circulation.

## Research Questions

- How much economic activity is generated by forestry and wood utilization?
- Which industries benefit from increased local timber use?
- How does local procurement affect the regional economy?
- How can forest-related industries contribute to regional revitalization?

## Research Topics

- Regional economic ripple effects
- Forestry and timber industries
- Local procurement
- Employment and income generation
- Supply chains
- Regional revitalization

## Methods

We use input-output analysis, economic ripple-effect analysis, supply-chain analysis, statistical data, and interviews with local stakeholders.
