## Research Philosophy

Forests provide far more than timber. They generate environmental, economic, social, and cultural value that supports sustainable communities.

Our laboratory studies how these values can be measured, visualized, and utilized to reconnect forests with people's everyday lives.

## Research Methods

Our research integrates methods from both natural and social sciences.

- 🌲 Forest ecology and field surveys
- 🪵 Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)
- 🌍 Greenhouse gas accounting
- 📈 Input–output and regional economic analysis
- 🛰 GIS and spatial analysis
- 🍄 Fungal and DNA analysis
- 📝 Questionnaire and interview surveys
- 🤝 Collaborative research with local communities

---

## Current Projects

Current research topics include:

- Environmental performance of wood utilization
- Carbon storage and climate benefits of wood products
- Regional economic impacts of forestry and timber industries
- Sustainable management of satoyama landscapes
- Biodiversity and fungal communities
- Community participation in forest management
