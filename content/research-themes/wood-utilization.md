## Overview

Wood utilization can contribute to climate change mitigation, regional industries, and sustainable forest management.

Our laboratory evaluates the environmental impacts and benefits of wood products throughout their life cycles, from forest management and harvesting to manufacturing, use, recycling, and disposal.

## Research Questions

- What are the greenhouse gas emissions associated with wood products?
- How much carbon is stored in long-lived wood products?
- Under what conditions does wood substitution reduce environmental impacts?
- How can local wood utilization support sustainable forest management?

## Research Topics

- Life Cycle Assessment
- Greenhouse gas accounting
- Carbon storage in wood products
- Material and energy substitution
- Local timber utilization
- Circular use of wood resources

## Methods

We use Life Cycle Assessment, carbon accounting, material flow analysis, and scenario analysis to evaluate the environmental performance of wood utilization.
