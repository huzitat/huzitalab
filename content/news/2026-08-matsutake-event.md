We will hold the **Nekoyama Matsutake Exploration Walk** on **Sunday, October 11, 2026**, at Nekoyama Sozo-no-Mori Park in Ueda, Nagano.

The event will take participants through the satoyama forest of Nekoyama, where matsutake mushrooms were once harvested. While walking through the forest, we will explore the ecology of the area and learn about the environmental conditions associated with matsutake mushrooms and the relationship between satoyama management and forest ecosystems.

The event is open to anyone interested in forests, satoyama landscapes, mushrooms, and local nature.

**Date:** Sunday, October 11, 2026
**Time:** 10:00–12:00
**Venue:** Nekoyama Sozo-no-Mori Park
**Participation fee:** Free
