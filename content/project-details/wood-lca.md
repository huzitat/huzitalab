## Overview

Wood utilization can contribute to climate change mitigation through carbon storage, material substitution, and the sustainable use of forest resources.

However, its environmental performance depends on multiple stages, including forest management, harvesting, transportation, processing, use, reuse, and disposal.

This project evaluates the environmental impacts and potential benefits of wood utilization using life cycle approaches.

## Objectives

- Quantify greenhouse gas emissions associated with wood products
- Evaluate carbon storage throughout product life cycles
- Compare wood-based products with alternative materials
- Identify opportunities to reduce environmental impacts
- Support environmentally responsible wood utilization

## Research Activities

The project examines environmental impacts across the wood supply chain.

Research activities include:

- Collection of forestry and wood-processing data
- Life cycle inventory development
- Greenhouse gas accounting
- Carbon balance assessment
- Comparison of alternative production and utilization scenarios
- Evaluation of recycling, reuse, and end-of-life options

## Methods

The project uses methods such as:

- Life Cycle Assessment
- Carbon footprint analysis
- Greenhouse gas accounting
- Scenario analysis
- Material flow analysis
- Statistical analysis

## Expected Outcomes

This research aims to provide quantitative information that supports:

- Low-carbon wood production
- Sustainable material selection
- Improved forest and wood-product management
- Regional climate strategies
- Evidence-based environmental decision-making
